## 7. Platform notes

**Clutch** (clutch.co)
- Profile ranking depends mostly on **verified client reviews**, which Clutch collects by phone or online form from the client. Aim for three to five to start.
- Choose service lines from section 5. Clutch shows "Min project size" and "Avg hourly rate" on the card; leave them for the team to decide.

**GoodFirms** (goodfirms.co)
- Similar fields to Clutch. Lists agencies by service and by country, so choose "India" and the lead generation categories.
- Reviews can be submitted by clients through a link you send them.

**Sortlist** (sortlist.com)
- Organised around projects and briefs. Add the portfolio items from section 6 as projects, each with one image (a results graphic or the case study's share card).
- Sortlist matches agencies to incoming briefs, so the service and industry choices matter more than the description.

**Google Business Profile** (business.google.com)
- Category: **Marketing agency** as primary; add "Business to business service" if available.
- Use the BKC address exactly as above. Google usually verifies by postcard, phone or video.
- Service area: leave it to the address. Do not add other cities as locations.
- Use the short description (section 3) and add the seven services as "Services".
