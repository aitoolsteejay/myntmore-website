## 1. Executive Summary and Critical Finding

### The 9 "Noindexed" Case Studies: Do NOT Remove from Sitemap
- **GSC Status:** "Submitted URL marked 'noindex'"
- **Codebase Reality:** Every single one of these 9 pages has `<meta name="robots" content="index, follow" />`, a self-referencing canonical, and valid JSON-LD schema. None of them contain a `noindex` directive.
- **Why GSC Reports This:** This is **stale data from 10 to 11 October 2026** when Google first crawled these URLs during initial drafting. Google dropped them and deprioritized re-crawling them.
- **Action:** Keep them in the sitemap. Do a **"Test Live URL"** in Search Console and click **"Request Indexing"** to overwrite Google's stale October cache.

### The 7 "Duplicate / Canonical" Pages: Stale Foreign Canonical Issue
- **GSC Status:** "Duplicate without user-selected canonical" or "Google chose different canonical than user"
- **Key URLs:** `/services` (last crawled **27 July 2026**, 115 days ago), `/services/gtm-strategy` (last crawled **30 August 2026**).
- **Codebase Reality:** In our code and HTML, `/services` has `<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.myntmore.com/services" />` and `<meta name="robots" content="index, follow" />`.
- **Why GSC Reports This:** In July and August 2026, foreign scraper domains (such as `747live.bet` and `marcustheatres.com`) scraped the site, and Google mistakenly assigned canonical ownership to external domains. Because Google has not re-crawled `/services` since 27 July, it is still evaluating the page using the 4-month-old stale crawl.
- **Action:** Open GSC URL Inspection for `https://www.myntmore.com/services`, click **"Test Live URL"** (forces live fetch), verify Google sees the user-declared canonical, and click **"Request Indexing"**. Repeat for `/services/gtm-strategy`.
