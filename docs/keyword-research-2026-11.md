# Keyword and AI-prompt research, November 2026

What Myntmore should rank for in search and be named for in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity: every city permutation, every ICP permutation, ICP × city, and a 63-prompt library for the monthly AI visibility check.

All of it is generated by `npm run keywords:build` (`scripts/generate-keyword-research.mjs`). The script takes the 29 ICPs and their 2026 status from `lib/value-prop.json` (the `/value-prop` data). It then checks every phrase against the 1,769 keywords the site's pages already target. Edit the lists in the script, not the CSVs.

| File | Rows | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [`keywords/city-keywords.csv`](./keywords/city-keywords.csv) | 315 | 15 service phrasings × 24 cities (the 8 with pages, 11 more Indian cities, 5 international), with spelling variants (Bangalore, Gurgaon, Delhi NCR, Baroda, Cochin) |
| [`keywords/icp-keywords.csv`](./keywords/icp-keywords.csv) | 129 | Commercial and "how do I" phrasings for all 29 ICPs |
| [`keywords/icp-city-keywords.csv`](./keywords/icp-city-keywords.csv) | 75 | ICP × city, only where that ICP clusters in that city (IT in Bengaluru/Pune/Noida, exporters in Rajkot/Ludhiana/Coimbatore, pharma in Hyderabad/Ahmedabad/Vadodara, …) |
| [`ai-visibility-prompts.csv`](./ai-visibility-prompts.csv) | 63 | Prompts to check in the four AI engines: 16 monthly, 47 quarterly |

Every keyword row has a **status**:
- **targeted**: a page already lists it.
- **mapped**: an existing page is the right one to rank. Add the phrase to that page's `keywords` and work it into the copy or an FAQ.
- **gap**: no page fits. The target column names the page to build.

Each row also has a **priority**:
- **P1**: a core or retain ICP, or a city that already has a page.
- **P2**: a test or subsegment ICP, or a tier-2 Indian city.
- **P3**: a deprioritised ICP or an international city.

**No search volumes.** No keyword tool was connected when this was built. Before building any "gap" page, check volumes in a keyword tool and look for impressions in Search Console's Performance report.

## What to do, in order

### 1. Build `/lp/accounting-cfo-firms` (ICP 24, core, no page today)
This is the biggest hole: 17 P1 keywords and a monthly AI prompt ("How can a virtual CFO firm get more clients?") with nowhere to land. `/lp/financial-services` targets lenders and NBFCs, a different buyer.

**Compliance:** ICAI's Code of Ethics bars chartered accountants in practice from soliciting clients. Do not pitch outreach to Indian CA firms. Position the page for virtual CFO, finance-operations and bookkeeping/accounting outsourcing firms, especially those selling to UK and US clients. ICP 25 has the same issue with law firms: Bar Council of India rules bar advocates from soliciting work.

### 2. Add the 248 "mapped" keywords to the pages that already exist
This is the cheapest win and needs no new pages. Most go to:

| Page | Mapped keywords |
|---|---|
| `/lp/manufacturers-exporters` | 33 |
| `/lp/agencies-it` | 30 |
| `/lp/saas-founders` | 26 |
| `/lp/recruitment-firms` | 18 |
| The 8 city pages | 10 to 21 each |

For each page, filter its CSV rows on `target_page`. Add the strongest phrases to its `keywords` array, which lives in the Keystatic CMS for city and landing pages. Turn the "how do I…" ones into FAQ answers: those are what AI engines quote.

Before adding, check `keyword-map.md` so no two pages share a primary keyword. City and ICP pages should keep their current primary and take these as secondary keywords.

### 3. Give ICP 1 (IT services) its own page
`/lp/agencies-it` serves two ICPs: agencies (ICP 4's cousin) and IT services firms (ICP 1, core). Thirty keywords are mapped onto it, which is more than one page can rank for well. Split it into `/lp/it-services` (ICP 1 and its subsegment ICP 20, R&D offshoring) and keep agencies on their own page.

### 4. Remove the Mumbai keyword from the homepage
The homepage's `keywords` include "b2b lead generation agency mumbai", which competes with `/b2b-lead-generation-mumbai`. Leave that one to the Mumbai page.

### 5. New city pages, only where there is something local to say
Each new city page needs real local content: the industries there, a relevant case study, local FAQs, like the existing 8. Without that, a new city page is a near-duplicate, which Google treats as a doorway page. Never build a page per service × city phrase; those all point at the one city page.

1. **Gurugram and Noida.** IT services, SaaS and GCC offices (core ICP 1, retain ICP 2), searched separately from "Delhi". Make sure the Delhi page links to both.
2. **The manufacturing and export belt: Rajkot, Ludhiana, Coimbatore, Surat, Vadodara.** Build these only alongside ICP 11 content (exporter case studies, trade-show timing).
3. **International: start with one UAE page** (Dubai plus Abu Dhabi), built on the UAE case studies at `/case-studies/markets/uae`. Add UK/London and Singapore once there is more case-study proof there.
4. **Jaipur, Kochi, Indore, Chandigarh:** later, if Search Console shows impressions for them.

### 6. Test ICPs: write the guide first, then the page
VC portfolio GTM (5), GCC providers (10), logistics (12), executive coaches (15) and corporate gifting (16) are all under test. For each, first publish one guide answering its "how do I" question, for example "How do corporate gifting companies get B2B orders?". That ranks, gets cited by AI engines, and tests demand. Build the landing page only once the ICP test is confirmed.

### 7. Deprioritised ICPs: track, don't build
The 15 P3 rows for ICPs 14, 18, 19, 21, 22, 23 and 26 to 29 stay in the CSV so they can be reopened. They have no target page on purpose.

## AI prompts

[`ai-visibility-prompts.csv`](./ai-visibility-prompts.csv) lists, for each prompt, the page that *should* be cited. If an engine answers without naming Myntmore, that page is where to strengthen the answer.

- **Monthly (16 prompts).** Asked in all four engines and logged in [`ai-visibility-tracker.csv`](./ai-visibility-tracker.csv), now 64 rows. They cover:
  - the original 9, unchanged so months stay comparable
  - best LinkedIn outreach agency in India
  - best lead generation company in Bangalore
  - the four core ICPs' "how do I get clients" questions
  - "Myntmore reviews"
- **Quarterly (47 prompts).** Ask a quarter of them each month, or all in one sitting each quarter. They cover:
  - more "best agency" lists
  - the other cities and Dubai
  - every test ICP's question
  - how-to questions (deliverability, LinkedIn limits, whether cold email is legal in India)
  - comparisons (vs in-house SDR, vs Belkins, Indian vs US agencies)
  - cost and reputation

"Alternatives to Belkins" and "Indian vs US outbound agency" have no page yet ("NEW: comparison page"). An honest comparison page covers both, and it is the format AI engines cite for those questions.

## Regenerating

```bash
npm run keywords:build
```

Run it after adding a page, changing a page's `keywords`, or changing an ICP's status in `/value-prop`; statuses update on the next run. To start a fresh monthly block in the tracker, copy the 64 rows and fill in the month. `--write-tracker` only rewrites the tracker while it is still empty.
